Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

The short version

  • Your measurements are stored locally on your device.
  • No account, no backend, and no internet permission in the app.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, in the app or on this website.
  • We never receive, sell or share your data, because it never reaches us.
  • You can delete everything at any time.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the BodyMetric Android app (package works.foundy.bodymetric, the “app”) and to this website (the “website”). BodyMetric is a personal body metrics and health calculator that gives estimates and educational information based on measurements you enter yourself.

“We” and “us” mean Foundy Works, the developer of BodyMetric. Contact details are in section 13.

2. What we collect: nothing

The app works without an account and has no backend. It does not send your health data, measurements or any other personal information to us or to anyone else. We have no copy of your data and cannot see, recover or restore it.

  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs are included in the app.
  • The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is technically unable to send your data over the network.
  • The only network-related feature is the optional in-app purchase described in section 5. Google Play handles it, not BodyMetric.

3. What is stored on your device

Everything you enter stays on your phone. Every field is optional.

Data is kept in the app’s private storage using a local Room database and DataStore preferences. Other apps cannot access it. The app is configured to exclude its data from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so your measurements are not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, address, location, contacts or photos.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making. We never have access to it in the first place.

5. Optional “Support BodyMetric” purchase

BodyMetric is free. You can optionally make a one-time “Support BodyMetric” in-app purchase, which unlocks extra themes. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing, and the app declares the com.android.vending.BILLING permission for this reason.

  • Google handles payment and account details under Google’s Privacy Policy. We never see your payment information.
  • The app only stores, on your device, whether the purchase is owned. No measurements or health data are involved in the purchase.
  • This flag is kept separate from your health data, so Delete all data does not remove a purchase you have paid for. Google Play can also restore it.

6. Exporting your data

You can optionally export a CSV file or a PDF report with your profile summary, current metrics, goal and progress history. The export is created on your device and saved only to the location you choose through the system file picker. Your nickname is not included. Once you save or share an exported file, it is outside BodyMetric. Its privacy then depends on where you keep it and who you share it with.

7. Your control: access, correction and deletion

  • See and correct your data at any time in Profile, Progress and History.
  • Delete single items such as a measurement or saved result.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Delete all data. You will be asked to confirm. This permanently removes your profile, measurements, history and settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Your data exists only on your device, so you exercise these rights directly in the app. We hold nothing we could provide, correct or delete on your behalf.

8. This website

This website only provides information about BodyMetric and links to Google Play. It has no forms or accounts, and it sets no cookies. It uses no analytics or tracking. It loads the Plus Jakarta Sans font from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests it from Google’s servers. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of request, to operate and secure the site.

9. Security

Your data sits in app-private storage protected by Android. Anyone who can unlock your phone may be able to open the app and see your data. Use a screen lock and keep your device updated.

10. Not a medical device

BodyMetric is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses. Results such as BMI, body fat, BMR, TDEE, calorie targets, water intake, waist-to-height ratio and goal dates are estimates based on published formulas and the values you enter.

BodyMetric provides estimates for informational and educational purposes only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Individual health and nutritional needs vary. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

11. Children

BodyMetric is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and its categories and formulas (such as BMI categories and calorie guidance) are designed for adults. They are not appropriate for children or teenagers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the app does not send data to us.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and publish the new version at this address. If a change is significant, for example if a future feature needed to send data off your device, we will explain it in the app before it takes effect. Any such feature would be optional and would ask for your consent first.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or BodyMetric’s privacy practices can be sent to dewa.m@quartexsoftware.com.
